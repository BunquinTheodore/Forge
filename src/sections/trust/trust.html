Trust and governance

Clear roles. Clear boundaries.

Open a role to see what it does and what it does not do.

Role Boundary

  1. Convening, preparation, facilitation and follow-up. No implied endorsement or investment guarantee.

    Does

    • Convenes
    • Prepares
    • Facilitates
    • Follows up

    Does not

    • Imply endorsement
    • Guarantee investment

  2. Independent judgment on investment, pricing, diligence and acceptable risk.

    Does

    • Judges investment independently
    • Sets pricing
    • Runs its own diligence
    • Decides what risk is acceptable

    Does not

    • Delegate investment judgment

  3. Information and assessment support; not automatically an investor or guarantor.

    Does

    • Supports with information
    • Supports with assessment

    Does not

    • Become an investor automatically
    • Become a guarantor automatically

  4. A future, separately structured role if a viable legal and financial mechanism is developed.

    Does

    • A possible future role, structured separately

    Does not

    • Exist until a viable legal and financial mechanism is developed

  5. Retain responsibility for disclosures, decisions, IP and agreements.

    Does

    • Owns its disclosures
    • Owns its decisions
    • Keeps its IP
    • Owns its agreements

    Does not

    • Transfer IP automatically

Principles

  • Consent-based information sharing
  • Conflicts disclosed
  • No pay-to-match
  • No automatic transfer of IP
  • Legal and regulatory review where required
