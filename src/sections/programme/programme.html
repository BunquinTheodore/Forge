Afternoon: the Adaptive Startup Workshop

Selected ventures move from presentation to structured working conversations around what they need to progress.

Bring the venture together with relevant capability, market and capital perspectives.

Challenge assumptions, evidence, execution gaps and investment conditions.

Introduce a missing capability, partner, market route, milestone or financing path where useful.

Agree what happens after the room: pilot, diligence, introduction, development path, investment dialogue, or stop.

The aim is not to force a deal in one day. It is to discover which ventures deserve a credible next step.
