02 The problem

The problem is not visibility. It is the gap between opportunity and investability.

Opportunity Investability

Drag the handle across, or use the arrow keys, to close the gap.

The problem in one line

The ecosystem does not lack people, ideas or capital. It lacks a repeatable way to configure them, de-risk the opportunity and convert signals into owned next steps.

  • Founders are visible

    But investors still need clearer evidence, governance, capital sequence and execution conditions.

  • Investors see risk

    But not always the path, cost or capability needed to reduce it.

  • Capabilities exist

    But remain outside the deal or outside the country when the startup needs them most.

  • Events stop early

    They create contacts, not enough owned next steps and measurable continuation.
