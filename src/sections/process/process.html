Operating model

How FORGE works.

Recruit potential. Configure capability. Improve readiness. Test what deserves to continue.

  1. 01

    Recruit

    Founders, startups, university teams and credible pre-company ideas.

  2. 02

    Profile

    Problem, evidence, team, need, capability, capital.

  3. 03

    Qualify

    Substance, clarity and actionability. Not endorsement.

  4. 04

    Configure

    Temporary cells around actual needs and missing capabilities.

  5. 05

    De-risk

    Make gaps and investor conditions legible.

  6. 06

    Convert

    Diligence, pilot, partnership, capital sequence or stop.

Solution architecture

A 90-day investment compound.

The public event is the MVP. The post-event compound is the validation cycle.

  1. FORGE PH

    Brand and platform for cross-border venture configuration.

  2. FORGE DAY

    The physical front-end: risk intelligence, showcases, tables and deal rooms.

  3. FORGE NEXT

    90-day post-event compound: measure, reconfigure or stop.

Working Cell rule

Need + complementary capability + willingness + possible action.

Switch on each condition. A cell only forms when all four are present.
