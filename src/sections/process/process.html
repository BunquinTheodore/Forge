Operating model

How FORGE works.

Recruit potential. Configure capability. Improve readiness. Test what deserves to continue.

  1. 01

    Recruit

    Founders, startups, university teams and credible ideas.

  2. 02

    Profile

    Problem, evidence, team, need, capital.

  3. 03

    Qualify

    Substance and clarity. Not endorsement.

  4. 04

    Configure

    Temporary cells around real needs.

  5. 05

    De-risk

    Make gaps and investor conditions legible.

  6. 06

    Convert

    Diligence, pilot, partnership, capital or stop.

Solution architecture

A 90-day investment compound.

  1. FORGE PH

    The cross-border venture platform.

  2. FORGE DAY

    The public event and MVP: showcases, tables and deal rooms.

  3. FORGE NEXT

    90-day post-event compound: measure, reconfigure or stop.
