The Italy-Philippines corridor

A business corridor for international startups.

Presented by the Italian Chamber of Commerce in the Philippines, FORGE PH 2027 opens the Philippine market to international ventures.

  • 01

    International business connections

    FORGE brings together international startups, Philippine enterprises, professional capabilities and investors, so that connections made on the day can become business opportunities.

  • 02

    Market entry

    For startups entering the Philippine market, the forum offers a starting point: market entry and industry sessions, startup case studies and cross-industry working tables.

  • 03

    The Italy-Philippines corridor

    The corridor joins Italian and international innovation with Philippine ventures, and brings new capabilities and business opportunities into the local ecosystem.

  • 04

    The future international platform

    International FORGE is the horizon: a global platform built to expand connections across international capital, ventures, markets and capabilities.
