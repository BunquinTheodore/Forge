From FORGE 01 to FORGE 02

FORGE is designed to learn.

  1. FORGE 01 Formation

    A first cohort of ventures, investors and capabilities meets, works and produces real next steps.

  2. Real-world interval

    Projects develop outside the event. Some advance, some change, some stop, and new needs emerge.

  3. FORGE 02 Continuation

    A strong returning core reconvenes, enriched only by the new capabilities required by what emerged.

FORGE 02 is not simply another edition. It starts from the evidence and unfinished work of FORGE 01.

Turning event activity into useful continuity

The Generative Recap

A conventional recap records what happened. FORGE aims to produce a structured, AI-assisted recap that helps participants understand what changed and what may deserve attention next.
