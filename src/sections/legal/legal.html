Who we are

FORGE PH 2027 is presented by the Italian Chamber of Commerce in the Philippines, Inc. (ICCPI). ICCPI decides how the information you give on this website is used.

What we collect

When you register, we ask for your name, email, mobile number, job title, organization, country, business category, sector and your interest in FORGE activities. Each form adds a few answers of its own:

  • Conference pass: the number of participants and, if you wish, their names.
  • Exhibition booth: your website, the booth package, what you will exhibit and any special requirements.
  • Workshop and FD90: how you would take part, your venture stage, a summary of your venture or what you bring, and the support you are looking for.

You can also add notes. We do not collect payment details through this website.

Why we use it

We use your information to register you, confirm your place, contact you about FORGE PH 2027 (including payment arrangements), plan the event, and follow up on the workshop, FD90 and FORGE NEXT if you asked to take part. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

Where it is kept and who can see it

  • Registrations are saved in a Google Sheet managed by ICCPI and shared with authorized ICCPI team members.
  • A notification email with your answers goes to ICCPI's registration team, and a confirmation email goes to you. Both are sent through the email service Resend.
  • This website is hosted on Vercel, which processes requests in order to deliver the pages and receive your registration.
  • We do not sell your information. We will not share it with other organizations without telling you first, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to run FORGE PH 2027 and its follow up, and then delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you may ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to how it is used, or withdraw your consent. Write to info@iccpi.org.ph or f.calabretta@iccpi.org.ph. You also have the right to complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update this page and the date above.
