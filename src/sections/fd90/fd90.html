FD90

From business connections to development pathways.

The forum is one day. FD90 is the 90-day process in which selected ventures configure, test and develop what the day started.

  1. Day 0

    Configuration

    Configuration, evidence, conditions and the next action are written down.

  2. Day 10

    Activation

    Did the pilot, diligence or financing step begin?

  3. Day 45

    Evidence

    What proof, capability or access changed?

  4. Day 90

    Reassessment

    Continue, reconfigure, seek capital or stop.

Milestones are indicative and will be confirmed with the selected ventures.

FORGE NEXT

Advance partnerships and business outcomes.

After the 90 days, the work continues as FORGE NEXT: a post-event cycle to measure, reconfigure or stop, and to move promising partnerships toward real business outcomes.
