The proposition

What FORGE brings to the market

Many promising ventures do not fail because the idea is weak. They stall because the right combination of evidence, capability, market access, capital and confidence has not yet formed.

  • Make the venture legible

    Clarify what exists, what is proven, what is missing and what the next meaningful step should be.

  • Bring the right people around it

    Connect founders with relevant investors, corporates, experts, accelerators and institutions.

  • Turn conversation into action

    Use structured working sessions to identify pilots, diligence steps, introductions, capabilities and investment conditions.

FORGE is not another pitch day. It is designed to improve the quality of the next decision.

Pre-forum preparation

FORGE starts before October 29

  1. Selected ventures are identified through the ICCPI network, partners, universities, accelerators and open outreach.

  2. A focused pre-event dialogue clarifies the venture, current stage, evidence, needs and ambitions.

  3. Relevant information is structured so investors and capability partners can understand the opportunity efficiently.

  4. Potential conversations and useful counterparties are identified before the room opens.
