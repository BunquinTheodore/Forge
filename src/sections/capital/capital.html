COMMERCIAL FRAMEWORK

Capital is only one part of the equation.

  • Smart capital

    Financial capital plus expertise, technology, market access and relationships.

  • Risk intelligence

    Better structured information and risk dialogue can help investors understand what is known, unknown and still to be demonstrated.

  • Risk sharing

    Where appropriate and legally structured, a separate third party may in future share a defined portion of eligible financing risk.

FORGE does not guarantee investment and does not remove investor judgment. It creates a better environment in which risk, capability and opportunity can be discussed.

A risk-intelligence platform can add value without becoming the investor or the guarantor.

Help identify the information that makes early-stage and operating startups easier to assess.
